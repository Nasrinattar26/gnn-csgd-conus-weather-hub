Evaluation scope and metric definitions

Common sample. Each duration uses its declared matched 2025 sample and common 0.25° CONUS verification grid. Counts represent gridpoint–forecast cases, not independent storms or observations.

Skill references. CRPSS = 1 − CRPS / CRPS(raw HRRR). BSS = 1 − BS / BS(shared 2024 validation climatology), evaluated on the 2025 cases.

Calibration. ECE = Σ(nb/N)|p̄b − ōb|. The reliability penalty is Σ(nb/N)(p̄b − ōb)²; resolution is Σ(nb/N)(ōb − ō)². Neural forecasts use ten probability bins; raw HRRR uses its two endpoint probabilities. See the ECMWF verification notes for Brier components.

Discrimination. Published ROC AUC uses a 10,001-bin histogram method for the neural forecasts. AUC values alone cannot reconstruct ROC curves.

Further diagnostics. MAE/RMSE/bias/correlation for postprocessed amounts, categorical scores, FSS, PR AUC, predictive intervals/WIS, PIT, density curves and paired confidence intervals require additional matched evaluation outputs. They are not supplied by the current public GNN tables.
