Evaluation scope and metric definitions

Matched cases. The three systems are evaluated on the same 2025 cases within each duration: 38,888,560 cases for 6 hours, 33,987,536 for 12 hours, and 24,238,760 for 24 hours. Counts represent gridpoint–forecast cases, not independent storms. Durations are evaluated separately.

Continuous skill. CRPS measures predictive-distribution error in millimeters; lower is better. CRPSS = 1 − CRPS / CRPS(raw HRRR). Raw HRRR's deterministic CRPS also equals its MAE.

Threshold skill. Events are precipitation strictly greater than the selected amount. Brier score measures probability error. BSS = 1 − BS / BS(shared 2024 validation climatology), evaluated on the matched 2025 cases.

ROC curves and AUC. The curves use cumulative event and non-event counts from the ten saved ANN/GNN probability bins; raw HRRR uses its exact 0/1 forecasts. Points correspond to bin cutoffs, and straight segments join consecutive points. They do not resolve rankings within a bin. The separate ROC AUC comparison retains the published 10,001-bin ANN/GNN scores. Areas under the displayed curves can therefore differ from those finer-resolution AUC values. ROC interpretation ↗

Calibration. ECE = Σ(nb/N)|p̄b − ōb|. Reliability penalty = Σ(nb/N)(p̄b − ōb)²; resolution = Σ(nb/N)(ōb − ō)². The last two are normalized by observed event uncertainty ō(1−ō). These binned diagnostics need not reproduce the exact unbinned Brier score. Brier components ↗

Score reductions. A positive reduction favors GNN-CSGD relative to ANN-CSGD. These are common-sample point estimates without confidence intervals.
